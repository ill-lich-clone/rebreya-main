The selected card definitions, helper code, English labels, and the two Grapple companion item templates are adapted from [Chris's Premades 1.5.44](https://github.com/chrisk123999/chris-premades/tree/1.5.44). The upstream MIT notice is in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

The selection corresponds to the legacy identifiers and rules editions on actors and scene tokens in the Rebreya world on 2026-11-02. The registry one directory above this file lists the entry points. It also includes the callbacks used by effects and secondary actions those cards create. Attack rolls, saving throws, dice, and document ownership continue to use Foundry, dnd5e, Midi-QOL, and DAE.

Local adapters handle modern Foundry placement, duration and change fields, authenticated requests to an owner, and existing Rebreya reaction and spell contracts. The source modules are maintained here as ES modules, independently of any installed CPR package.
